About

WarCritters is an animal battle simulator built in Bevy/Rust. Draft two armies from a roster of dozens of species, set them loose in an arena, and watch the fight play out — or drop into any critter on your side and fight alongside it in first or third person.

It is a physics game first. Every unit is a real body with a real hitbox: it climbs terrain, gets shoved, flinches from hits, topples when it dies, and piles up where it falls.

What is in it

  • A large roster — wolves, bears, horses, sharks, dinosaurs, and armoured humanoids, each with its own size, health, speed and pair of special abilities.
  • Possess anything — take direct control of any unit in your army, in first or third person, and hand it back to the AI when you are done.
  • Real weapons — a ten-weapon arsenal from assault rifles to bolt-action snipers and a katana, each with its own iron sights, ballistics and recoil.
  • Commander mode — pull back to an RTS camera, give stance orders, place buildings and scatter power-ups before the fight.
  • The Armory — a dedicated firing range with every weapon and modifier on hand, for players who want to learn the guns properly.
