About

WarCritters is a physics-driven team battle-arena. Draft a squad of low-poly animals against a gold budget, place your buildings, and command the whole army from an RTS-style overhead view — then double-click any of your units to possess it and fight in first or third person, right in the middle of the brawl.

Every clash runs on real rigid-body physics: projectiles arc, melee lands with impact, and bodies tumble across the terrain. It's built in Bevy (Rust) with the avian3d physics engine, the same toolchain behind Rad Winds.

An early single-player vertical slice — systems, art, and balance are all in active development, so expect placeholder visuals and rapid change.

What's in the slice

  • Animal roster with gold-budget army drafting
  • Overhead RTS command — select, group, and order your units
  • Possess any unit for first / third-person hero combat
  • Ranged weapons, projectiles, and physics-driven melee
  • Health, teams, and damage with AI-controlled enemy packs
  • Death & respawn, live score HUD, and a win → end-of-match flow
  • Terrain arenas with a day/night cycle and weather
