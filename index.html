WarCritters In development

A physics team battle-arena: draft an army of low-poly animals, command them from above, then double-click to possess one and fight in the thick of it. Built in Bevy/Rust + avian3d.

A year in the canopy

The Wartburg TN level grows a procedural Appalachian forest from real USGS LiDAR canopy-height data. Every species carries a four-season material set, so the same hillside greens up in spring, deepens through summer, turns in autumn, and drops to bare branches under winter snow — while the conifers stay evergreen.
